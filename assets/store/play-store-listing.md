## Full description — ENGLISH, version A (privacy and control)

KeepTaste is a recipe manager that runs entirely on your phone. No account, no subscription, no cloud, no ads, no tracking.

YOUR RECIPES STAY WITH YOU
Everything is stored locally, in your phone's memory. The app works on a plane, in a basement and with no signal. The only time it reaches the network is when it loads a page from a link you paste yourself during import — nothing is sent or synced anywhere.

ORGANIZE INTO COOKBOOKS
Group recipes into cookbooks — weekday dinners, desserts, holiday cooking. Browse them as tiles and search by title, diacritics included. Recipes you haven't made yet can be marked "To try" and stay in their own section until you cook them.

ADD RECIPES WITHOUT RETYPING THEM
- From a link — the app reads the recipe data from the page and skips the ads and the author's life story
- From pasted text — ingredients and instructions are split automatically
- From a photo — text recognition runs on the device, offline (a screenshot, a page of a cookbook)
- From handwriting — the app prepares the photo and a ready-made prompt for your own AI app; KeepTaste sends nothing by itself
- Straight from the Share menu in your browser or notes app

Every import lands in the form first — you review and fix it before anything is saved.

BUILT FOR COOKING
The screen stays awake while you read a recipe, and the A− / A+ buttons enlarge the text when the phone sits further down the counter. Photo, ingredients, instructions, times, servings and notes — empty fields are simply hidden.

SHOPPING LISTS
Send a recipe's ingredients to a list in one tap, or paste a whole block of products from a message. Tick things off in the shop, keep several lists at once, share a list as plain text.

BACKUPS YOU ACTUALLY HAVE
- A complete .zip backup including photos — with full restore
- Automatic backups to a folder you choose on the phone, keeping the last few versions
- Export of the whole collection to Markdown — readable in any editor and importable back
- Share a cookbook as a PDF, a single recipe as text

LOOK AND LANGUAGE
Light, dark or system theme. Interface in English and Polish.

NO SURPRISES
No account, no server, no analytics, no ads, no subscription. On first launch you get a few sample recipes so you can see how it works — they disappear once you add your own.

## Full description — ENGLISH, version B (practical, benefit-led)

Your recipes are in browser bookmarks, in screenshots, in messages from your mum and on a card in a drawer. KeepTaste brings them together in one place — on your phone, offline, with no account to create.

COLLECT RECIPES FROM WHERE THEY ALREADY ARE
Paste a blog link and the app pulls out the ingredients and the method without the ads and the holiday-in-Italy intro. Paste text from a message. Photograph a page of a cookbook — the text is recognized directly on the device, with no internet. Handwriting? The app prepares the photo and a ready-made prompt for your own AI app, and you paste the answer back. You can also send a recipe to KeepTaste straight from the system Share menu.

Before anything is saved you see the form and can fix every field.

ARRANGE IT YOUR WAY
Cookbooks for weekday dinners, desserts and holidays. A tile view with photos, title search that handles diacritics. Recipes marked "to try" stay separate until you actually cook them.

COOK WITHOUT TOUCHING YOUR PHONE
The screen stays awake in the recipe view, and one button enlarges the text so you can read it from the other side of the counter. Headings, bold text and bullet lists in the ingredients work exactly as you type them.

SHOPPING WITHOUT RETYPING
Send a recipe's ingredients to a shopping list, or paste a whole block of products at once. Tick them off in the shop, run several lists at the same time, and send a list to someone as plain text.

DON'T LOSE WHAT YOU'VE COLLECTED
A complete .zip backup holds your recipes together with their photos and restores the whole library on a new phone. Backups can be written automatically to a folder you choose, keeping the last few versions. You can also export everything to Markdown — plain text files you can open anywhere and import back. Share a cookbook as a PDF, a single recipe as a message.

SMALL THINGS THAT MATTER
Light and dark themes, English and Polish interface, undo for an accidental delete, swipe actions in lists, and no empty dashes where a field wasn't filled in.

WHAT ISN'T HERE
Accounts, subscriptions, ads, analytics and the cloud. Your data sits on your device; the app reaches the internet only when you paste a recipe link yourself.

## Privacy policy
Required — publish at GitHub Pages or Notion before submitting.
Key points to note: no data collection, no accounts, no analytics, all data local on device.
**Data safety accuracy:** the app is not fully network-free — importing a recipe performs a
user-initiated `fetch` of the URL the user pastes, straight to that site (no backend, nothing
collected or transmitted by us). OCR runs on-device; the "Transcribe with AI" mode makes no
network call at all (it hands a photo and a prompt to the user's own AI app via the share sheet).
Do not claim "never connects to the internet" in the listing.
