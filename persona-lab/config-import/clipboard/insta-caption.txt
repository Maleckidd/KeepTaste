the 20 min miso butter noodles everyone keeps asking for 🍜✨ saving you the scroll, here's the whole thing!!

you need:
200g egg noodles
3 tbsp butter
1 tbsp white miso
2 garlic cloves, grated
1 tbsp soy sauce
1 tsp honey
handful of spring onions
chilli crisp to finish 🌶️

how:
boil noodles, keep a mug of the water. melt butter in a pan with the garlic for 1 min, whisk in miso, soy + honey. toss noodles through with a splash of noodle water til glossy. top with spring onions + chilli crisp. serves 2 💛
